# Governance

Maintainers review consumer usefulness, correctness, privacy, accessibility,
sustainable cost and compatibility. Dependency or agent counts do not establish
quality; stars do not determine technical or legal decisions.

Version public contracts semantically and explain breaking migrations. Country
rules require official sources, effective intervals, exceptions and boundary
tests. AI-generated contributions receive the same review as any other work.

No sponsored rankings, undisclosed commercial preference or sale of traveler
data. Declare conflicts in proposals. Keep evidence private and examples
synthetic. Do not publish contributor identities without their explicit choice.

Security reports follow SECURITY.md; conduct concerns follow CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.
Maintainers should explain proportionate decisions and preserve factual records
without personal data. No uptime, support-response or legal-outcome SLA is promised.
