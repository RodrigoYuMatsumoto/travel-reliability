# Support

Start with documentation and the synthetic demo. Bugs need version, expected and
observed behavior, and a minimal synthetic reproduction. Rule proposals need
jurisdiction, official URL, effective interval, relevant facts and exceptions.
Never submit real travel documents for public debugging.

Maintainers provide software collaboration, not emergency/booking assistance,
a legal opinion or a promised refund. Seek immediate travel assistance from the
actual supplier or competent service. Community response depends on availability.

Disclose security problems through SECURITY.md, without public exploit details
or personal data.
