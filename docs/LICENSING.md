use the standard MIT permission and warranty terms in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).
The copyright block identifies the original repository; preserve the notices
when copying substantial portions. Attribution example:

```text
Based on Travel Reliability
https://github.com/RodrigoYuMatsumoto/travel-reliability
Copyright (c) 2026 Travel Reliability Contributors — MIT License
```

responsible for deployment, credentials, third-party terms, data and actions. The
license contains no-warranty and liability terms; it does not override mandatory
law. No payment, registration or exclusive model/provider is required by it.

The [Open Source Initiative MIT text](https://opensource.org/license/mit) was
checked on 2026-10-31. No anti-copying, model restriction or field-of-use clause
is added. The original repository URL accompanies the attribution block; this
is not a promise that every downstream interface will visibly display it.

Published versions through v0.3.0 retain their Apache-2.0 terms. The
[historical text](../LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt) is preserved, without rewriting tags.
Synthetic examples explicitly identified in NOTICE use CC0-1.0. Dependencies,
official materials and third-party datasets retain their own terms; the MIT
change does not relicense them. Generate and inspect the deployment SBOM.
