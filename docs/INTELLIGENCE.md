# Deterministic intelligence contracts

Architectural generation 3 extends the existing documentary core. It does not
certify zero hallucinations, provider access, personal eligibility or real-world
truth. The original nine planning IDs and expense settlement remain available.

Load one role, one workflow and its schema. Keep the complete loyalty graph in
the deterministic process. `travel_plan` adds `loyalty-arbitrage` and
`multimodal-stitching`; `travel_country_context` and `travel_audit_output` are
bounded local tools, not account or document connectors. Existing installations
must explicitly update their allowlist to expose these two additional tools.

## Loyalty

The graph distinguishes direct transfers, account links, vouchers and checkout
purchase. Unknown ratios are null, not 1:1. Every hop enforces known minimums,
caps, lot sizes and declared eligibility; a restricted direct edge does not ban
an unrelated indirect path. Operator declarations remain declarations.

The engine uses BigInt fractions, combined ratio/bonus floor at each hop and
conservative upward rounding only for displayed monetary units. It enumerates
simple paths for a fixed supplied amount, with hop/expansion caps. It does not
optimize split balances or assert a global optimum. Truncated coverage is explicit.

`sourcePoints` means **owned points consumed before checkout top-up**. Purchased
top-up points are added to the transferred balance, while their cash cost is
counted once. Owned points require a positive economic valuation or remain
unknown. The engine never assigns zero economic cost to an owned balance.
Promotional bonuses come from the versioned graph, never a host-supplied bonus.
Unknown fees, annual capacity, business calendars, account status, expiry and
award availability prevent a confirmed economic recommendation.

Public-source snapshots and operator proposals are different states. A populated
ratio or SHA does not grant `reviewed`. Unknown partner relationships remain
unresolved. Verify current operational terms before any real transfer.

## Multimodal routes

Nodes represent physical places. Different terminals require explicit surface
legs. Dates use IANA zones and reject ambiguous/nonexistent local times. Known
connection buffers are enforced; missing requirements keep a path conditional.
Separate tickets, positioning, baggage and hidden-city flags remain visible.
Known totals are segregated by currency, without inferred FX or guaranteed
contractual connection protection. Search runs only over supplied schedules.

## Country and cultural packs

Country, subdivision and municipality scopes compose without automatic overriding.
Contradictory active claims remain conflicts. Operational and cultural data are
separate from executable country legal rules. Cultural guidance is contextual,
not a nationality stereotype. Safety/app rankings need dated, scoped evidence;
none are invented by these schemas. Synthetic fixtures are never production facts.

## Evidence gate

`EvidenceReference` binds a source capture digest, exact short span, retrieval,
review deadline and typed assertion. An operator registry is separate from the
packet being checked. Caller-supplied reviewed flags cannot enroll evidence.
`CalculationReceipt` binds input/output digests, algorithm and dataset versions.
Calculation evidence proves a calculation over supplied inputs, not their truth.
MCP receipts are kept only in a bounded, fifteen-minute process-local map.
Country fact assertions bind the complete statement, context and typed value;
an unchanged typed value cannot authorize different prose. Calculation claims
must name the generating calculator and the `result` predicate. Captures have
not been promoted to reviewed evidence merely because their hashes are present.

Fact claims without a matching current assertion are blocked. Guidance and
hypotheses remain labeled. A critic LLM is advisory and cannot promote unsupported
facts. Free-form responses outside controlled interfaces are not intercepted.
MCP output follows the content policy without echoing rejected material.

The default connector policy remains **no automatic retry**. Any future retry
must be explicitly configured for one idempotent read, classified transient
failure, bounded backoff, deadline and total call budget. This release adds no
transfer, payment, account login, remote plugin or arbitrary-file capability.
