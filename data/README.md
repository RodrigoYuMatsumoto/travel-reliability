# Versioned loyalty proposal

`loyalty_graph.json` is a constrained operational **proposal**, not a certified
directory or current financial offer. Unspecified ratios, limits and calendars
are null. No personal account data is included. `loyalty_proposal_status.json`
records unresolved relationships; absence of an edge does not prove prohibition.

The structural data and original proposal metadata follow the repository MIT
license. External source content remains under its original terms. Only short
source spans, first-party URLs, retrieval dates and SHA-256 capture identifiers
are included; full source HTML is excluded. A digest identifies captured bytes,
not authorship, availability, accuracy, licensing permission or current terms.

`source_captured` is deliberately weaker than `reviewed`. One observed ratio
does not verify every eligibility rule, minimum, fee, calendar, bonus or limit on
its edge. Account-link networks are not assumed to be complete graphs.

Hotel credits, card benefits, vouchers, status privileges and companion passes
are distinct units and conditional entitlements. They are not inserted as miles
or cash-equivalent edges without a separate verified conversion contract.
Points-and-cash checkout is a caller-declared purchase, not a bank transfer.
No account login, balance fetch, points purchase, transfer or payment is performed.
